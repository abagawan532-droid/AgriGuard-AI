📐 Scientific & Forestry Formulations

1. Huber's Tree Stem Volume Formula:

V = π × (DBH / 200)² × Height × Form Factor (0.65)

2. IPCC Carbon Sequestration Model:

CO₂ Equivalent = Wood Volume × Density (650 kg/m³) × 0.5 (C fraction) × (44/12)

3. Agro-Economic Net Return:

Net Profit = (Yield × Price) - (Seed + Fertilizer + Irrigation + Labor)

🛡️ Ethical AI & Limitations

  • Tree age cannot be accurately determined from a 2D photograph alone. Precise dendrochronological dating requires tree core sampling or verifiable historical planting documentation.
  • Agricultural disease predictions should be regarded as preliminary decision support. Always confirm before purchasing restricted systemic agro-chemicals.
  • All commercial crop pricing and timber rates are indicative market averages and can fluctuate based on localized mandi market conditions.
